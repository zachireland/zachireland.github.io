Research Positions

Research Interests

My current interests include the James Webb Space Telescope’s Mid-Infrared Instrument (MIRI-MRS), particularly its observations of the spectra and stellar winds of Wolf-Rayet stars orbiting Sagittarius A* in the Galactic Center and their role in feeding the central black hole.

I value breadth in my scientific endeavours and have pursued research across a wide range of fields, including astrophysics at CITA, quantum computing and biophysics at IQC, quantum condensed matter at the University of Toronto, inflationary cosmology at WITP, nuclear and particle physics at the University of Winnipeg, and materials science at the University of Manitoba.

My long-term career goals include the completion of an MSc and PhD, subsequently pursuing an academic career in theoretical and observational astrophysics and cosmology.
